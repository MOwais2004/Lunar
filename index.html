How it works

Three steps between an alert and your sleep.

Lunar sits between your monitoring and your phone. Nothing else changes.

01 / INGEST

Everything lands in one stream

Point Datadog, Sentry, CloudWatch, Grafana and your own webhooks at Lunar. Setup is a URL and a token, not a migration.

02 / TRIAGE

Lunar reads it against your runbook

It collapses duplicates, correlates against the last deploy, checks dependent services, and matches the signature to a runbook you already wrote.

03 / RESOLVE OR WAKE

Most close themselves

When a runbook applies, Lunar runs it and logs what it did. When it doesn't, it pages the right person with the full trail attached.

Features

Smart deduplication

Questions

The ones everyone asks.

What happens if Lunar gets it wrong and suppresses something real?

Nothing is suppressed silently. Every decision is written to the incident log with the confidence score and the runbook that matched. You set the confidence floor for auto-resolution, and anything below it pages a human by default. Most teams start at 0.95 and relax it once they have seen a month of decisions.

Do we have to replace PagerDuty or Opsgenie?

No. Plenty of teams run Lunar in front of an existing paging tool for the first few months, so Lunar does the triage and hands the survivors over. When you trust it, you can let Lunar do the paging too and switch the old tool off.

How long does setup actually take?

The ingest side is a URL and a token per source, so under ten minutes. Runbook automation is worth more time: teams usually import three or four existing runbooks in the first week, then add one each time an incident repeats.

Can it run remediations against production?

Only the ones you explicitly grant. Actions are scoped with your own IAM roles, every run is logged and reversible, and you can set any runbook to propose-only so it writes out what it would do and waits for a human to approve.

What does it do with our alert data?

Alert content stays in your region and is never used to train shared models. Enterprise plans can keep ingest entirely inside your own VPC. Lunar is SOC 2 Type II and the report is available under NDA.
